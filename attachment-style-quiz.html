Attachment Style Quiz: Find Your Style in Love

Ten quick questions about closeness, space, and comfort in love.

You have felt it in your own way, that pull toward closeness or that need for a little space, and you have wondered what it says about you. This attachment style quiz gives you a simple way to look at those patterns without any judgment.

Attachment styles describe how we tend to act in close bonds. They grow from early life and from the relationships we have had since, and they shape how we ask for comfort, handle distance, and talk through hard days.

Answer each question with your first honest instinct. There are no right or wrong answers here, only clues about what helps you feel safe and loved.

Question 1 of 10

How it works

This attachment style quiz asks 10 short questions about daily moments in a relationship. Each answer points to one of four styles: secure, anxious, avoidant, or fearful-avoidant.

You will see one question at a time, with four options to pick from. Your result is the style you picked most often, and ties go to the style listed first in this order: secure, anxious, avoidant, fearful-avoidant.

Read your result as a starting point for a kind talk, not as a fixed label. People grow and change, and a style can shift as trust builds over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an attachment style?

+
An attachment style is a pattern in how you connect with a partner, including how you seek comfort, handle space, and respond to stress in the bond.

Is this attachment style quiz accurate?

+
It is a fun self-check, not a clinical test. It can spark useful insight, but it cannot diagnose anyone or replace a talk with a trained professional.

Can my attachment style change?

+
Yes, it can shift over time. New experiences, honest talks, and steady, kind relationships can all help you feel safer and more secure.

What if my result feels like a mix?

+
That is normal, and most people see parts of more than one style in themselves. Your top result shows your strongest pattern right now, not your whole story.
