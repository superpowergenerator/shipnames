Am I in Love Quiz: Find Out How You Feel

Ten honest questions about how you think, feel, and act around them.

You keep thinking about them, your mood lifts when they text, and you catch yourself smiling for no clear reason. You may wonder if it's love, a crush, or just the thrill of someone new, and that doubt can sit with you all day. This am i in love quiz helps you sort those feelings into words.

What your result means

Your total in this am i in love quiz runs from 0 to 30, and each answer scores 0 to 3 points based on how strongly it points to love. A score of 22 to 30 means love, a score of 12 to 21 means growing feelings, and a score of 0 to 11 means a crush or a close friendship so far. Those bands give you words for feelings that can feel hard to name.

Love often shows up in small signs, and this am i in love quiz looks at those signs one by one. You may want to share good news with them first, miss them when they're away, and care for them as they are, flaws and all. If you picture a future with them in it and it feels warm and right, that's a strong sign your feelings run deep. Picture hearing good news at work and reaching for your phone to tell them before anyone else. That urge to share first says a lot about where they sit in your life, and it sits close to what psychologists call limerence.

A middle score in this am i in love quiz often means your feelings are still growing, so don't rush to name them before they've had time to settle. You may enjoy their company, miss them a little when they're gone, and start to picture more, yet still feel unsure. That in-between place is normal, and the next section shows you what to do with it.

What to do with your result

Let your result from this am i in love quiz guide a gentle next step, not a rushed choice, and start with time together. Spend more days doing simple things side by side, like a walk, a meal, or a quiet evening talking, and watch how you feel after. If you leave those days lighter and glad you saw them, your feelings are likely growing in a good direction. Psychology Today sets out five ways to tell love from infatuation.

Talk honestly with a close friend first, if that helps you sort your thoughts, then talk with the person themselves when you're ready. You don't need a grand speech, since a simple line like I've enjoyed getting closer to you can open the talk. If your score sits in the middle, give it a few weeks and retake this am i in love quiz, then compare how your answers shift. Feelings can grow with shared days, and a lower score today doesn't close the door. To weigh the bond further, try the is he the one quiz or the attachment style quiz to see how you handle closeness. Those results can help you see the full picture.

What this quiz can't tell you

No quiz can decide if you're in love, and this am i in love quiz can't either, since only you can weigh what you feel day to day. It can help you notice your own feelings more clearly, and that clarity is a good place to start, yet it can't replace your own judgment. Use it as a mirror, not as a judge.

Don't answer with the answer you wish were true, since a score built on wishes won't help you decide anything real. Answer with your honest feelings, even when they feel messy or mixed, and let the total show where your heart seems to be right now. If you catch yourself picking the most loving option each time, pause and pick the one that fits your real week.
