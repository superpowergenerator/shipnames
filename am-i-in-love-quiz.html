Am I in Love Quiz: Find Out How You Feel

Ten honest questions about how you think, feel, and act around them.

You keep thinking about them, your mood lifts when they text, and you catch yourself smiling for no clear reason. This am i in love quiz helps you sort those feelings into words.

Love often shows up in small signs: wanting to share good news first, missing them when they are away, and caring for them as they are. This quiz looks at those signs one by one.

Answer with your honest feelings, not the answer you wish were true. Your total will point to where your heart seems to be right now.

Question 1 of 10

How it works

This am i in love quiz asks 10 questions, and each answer scores 0 to 3 points. Your total runs from 0 to 30, and you will see one question at a time.

Scores fall into three bands: 22 to 30 means love, 12 to 21 means growing feelings, and 0 to 11 means a crush or a close friendship so far.

Feelings can grow with time and shared days, so a lower score today does not close the door. Let the result guide a gentle next step, not a rushed choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this quiz score my answers?

+
Each answer scores 0 to 3 points, based on how strongly it points to love. Your total out of 30 decides which of the three readings you see.

Can a quiz tell me if I am in love?

+
No quiz can decide that for you. It can help you notice your own feelings more clearly, and that clarity is a good place to start.

What if my score is in the middle?

+
A middle score often means your feelings are still growing. Spend more time together, keep talking honestly, and see how you feel in a few weeks.

Is this quiz private?

+
Yes, your answers stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere. You can retake it whenever you like.
