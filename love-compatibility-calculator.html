How to read your score

The tool turns your two names into a fixed seed, so the same pair always lands on the same percentage. Alex and Emma will get the same score every time you run them, which keeps the game fair when friends compare. Add birthdays and the seed shifts a little, so the score can move a few points.

Scores of 80 and up read as a strong match, scores from 55 to 79 read as a good match with room to grow, and scores below 55 read as opposites who can still work. Each band comes with a short reading that fits the number, so read the words next to the score before you judge it. The love compatibility calculator bands are broad on purpose, since a name based score can't see your real life.

A low score means nothing bad on its own, and plenty of happy couples would land low on a name based score. What counts is how you talk, listen, and show up for each other, and no formula can measure that. Treat a low number as a joke to laugh at together, not as a warning sign. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the love compatibility calculator accurate?

+
No, it is for fun only. The score comes from your names through a fixed formula, and it does not test your real bond, values, or habits.

Why does the same pair always get the same score?

+
The score is built from a fixed seed made from the two names, so the same names always give the same result. That keeps the tool fair and repeatable.

Do birthdays change the score?

+
Yes, when you add them. Birthdays join the seed, so the score can shift a little. Leave them blank if you want a score from names alone.

What does a low score mean?

+
