How it works

The tool takes both names, puts them in the same order every time, and turns them into a fixed seed number. That seed sets the score, so the same pair always lands on the same percentage, and adding birthdays only changes the seed when you fill them in.

Scores of 80 and up read as a strong match, scores from 55 to 79 read as a good match with room to grow, and scores below 55 read as opposites who can still work. Each band comes with a short reading that fits the number.

Keep the result light and share it with your partner for a laugh. The best use of a love compatibility calculator is a fun prompt for a real talk, not a final say on how well you fit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the love compatibility calculator accurate?

+
No, it is for fun only. The score comes from your names through a fixed formula, and it does not test your real bond, values, or habits.

Why does the same pair always get the same score?

+
The score is built from a fixed seed made from the two names, so the same names always give the same result. That keeps the tool fair and repeatable.

Do birthdays change the score?

+
Yes, when you add them. Birthdays join the seed, so the score can shift a little. Leave them blank if you want a score from names alone.

What does a low score mean?

+
Nothing bad on its own. A low score here is a playful result, and plenty of happy couples would land low on a name-based score.
