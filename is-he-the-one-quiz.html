Is He the One Quiz: Check the Signs

Ten honest questions about trust, respect, and your future together.

You love him, and you also want to know if this bond has what it takes for the long run. This is he the one quiz looks at trust, respect, and shared plans, one honest question at a time.

The signs that matter most are often quiet ones: how he treats you when no one is watching, how he handles a hard talk, and whether you feel like yourself beside him.

Answer with what is true today, not what you hope will change. Your total will show where the bond stands and what deserves a kind talk next.

Question 1 of 10

How it works

This is he the one quiz asks 10 questions, and each answer scores 0 to 3 points. Your total runs from 0 to 30, and you will see one question at a time.

Scores fall into three bands: 22 to 30 means yes, 12 to 21 means real promise with things to talk about, and 0 to 11 means trust your gut and take your time.

No quiz can make a life choice for you. Use your result as a prompt for honest talks, and trust your own sense of how the relationship feels day to day.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this quiz score my answers?

+
Each answer scores 0 to 3 points, based on how strongly it points to trust, respect, and a shared future. Your total out of 30 decides your reading.

Can a quiz tell me if he is the one?

+
No quiz can decide that for you. It can help you weigh the signs you already see, and that clarity can guide your next talk.

What should I do if my score is low?

+
Take your time and trust your gut. Talk to someone you trust, and have an honest talk with him about what you need before you make any big choice.

Is this quiz private?

+
Yes, your answers stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere. You can retake it whenever you like.
