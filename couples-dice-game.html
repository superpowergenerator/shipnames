Couple Dice Game

Roll two dice, add them up, and let the total pick a sweet thing to do together.

Date nights stall when picking takes longer than doing. The couple dice game fixes that with one roll, one total, and one clear prompt you can act on right away.

We wrote a prompt for every total from 2 to 12, so no roll leaves you guessing. Roll the dice, read your prompt, and enjoy the moment it creates.

How to play

Tap Roll the dice in the couple dice game below. Two dice will show, the total will appear, and the matching prompt will show under the dice.

Follow the prompt together, then roll again for a new one. Every total has its own prompt, and a low roll can be just as sweet as a high one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play the couple dice game?

+
Roll two dice, add the faces together, and read the prompt for that total. Each total from 2 to 12 has its own prompt.

What happens if I roll the same total twice?

+
You get the same prompt again, and you can enjoy it a second time or roll again for a new total.

Can we play the dice game on a date night?

+
Yes. It works well as a date night starter because one roll picks the next thing to do, with no long debate.

Is the dice game free?

+
Yes. It is free, it runs in your browser, and there is nothing to download or sign up for.
