How it works

This wedding countdown takes your wedding date and counts the time left to it, updating every second. You will see days, hours, minutes, and seconds, all ticking down together.

The countdown starts at midnight on your wedding day, so the final hours lead right into the morning of the celebration. If the date has already passed, you will see a kind note asking for a future date.
