Marriage Compatibility Test

Answer 10 quick questions about life together and get a score out of 100, with a short reading for your result.

Marriage runs on daily habits more than grand gestures. How you handle money, chores, fights, and free weekends shapes the years ahead, and it's easy to skip those talks until a small clash turns big.

This marriage compatibility test asks 10 quick questions with three choices each. Answer honestly, one question at a time, and you'll get a score out of 100 plus a short reading that points at your strong spots and your gaps.

Treat the result as a starting point for a talk, not as a pass or fail mark. The best use of the score is to spot one habit you can improve together this week. And if you want a shared name for the two of you, the ship name generator can build one in seconds.

How it works

Each question covers one part of shared life, like money, chores, fights, family, and future plans. The first choice in each question scores 10 points, the middle choice scores 6, and the last choice scores 2, so the top score is 100.

The progress bar shows where you are in the test, and your answers stay on this page only. Nothing gets saved or sent anywhere, so you can answer with full honesty.

Scores of 80 and up read as a strong match, scores from 55 to 79 read as a good match with room to grow, and scores below 55 point at habits worth a calm talk. Use your marriage compatibility test result to pick one small change and try it together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the marriage compatibility test free?

+
Yes, it is free, and you can retake it as often as you like. There is no sign up, and your answers stay in your browser.

Should we take the test together?

+
Yes, that works best. Take it together and talk over each answer, or take it apart and compare scores after. Both ways can start a useful talk.

What does a low score mean?

+
It means a few daily habits need work, not that the marriage is doomed. Pick the question where you scored lowest and talk about one small change.

Is this test a real diagnosis?

+
No. It is a fun self-check built from common couple questions. For real help with a tough patch, talk to a licensed counselor together.
