Anniversary Calculator: Years, Months, and Days Together

Enter your date and see your time together in years, months, and days.

Some dates stay with you forever, and this anniversary calculator turns yours into a number you can celebrate. Enter the day you got together or married, and see how long you have shared, down to the days. It also finds your next anniversary and counts the days left to it, so you always know when the next celebration is coming.

How long have you been together?

Pick the day you got together or married, then tap the button. You'll see your time together in years, months, and days, plus your next anniversary.

Your result will show here. Pick a date from the past to get started.

How to get the most from your date

Enter the day you got together or the day you married, since either date works and each one tells a different story. The anniversary calculator counts full years first, then whole months, then the days left over, so the total reads like 3 years, 2 months, and 5 days. That shape feels more real than a bare day count.

Your next anniversary is the same month and day in the year ahead, unless it falls today or has already passed this year. If today is the day, the countdown shows 0 days, which calls for a celebration right now. Check the date twice before you share the result, since a wrong month gives you a wrong count.

A future date won't work here, since an anniversary marks a day that already happened. If you pick a date ahead of today, you'll see a kind note asking for a past date instead, so double check the year you typed. The tool keeps your date in your browser, and nothing gets sent anywhere or saved.

What to do with your result

Use the day count from the anniversary calculator to plan something that fits the milestone. A 100 day mark calls for a small treat, a 1,000 day mark calls for a night out, and a round year calls for something bigger. The wedding countdown keeps the wait fun when the next big date is still ahead.

Share the result as a card note or a caption, since a number like 5 years and 214 days feels more personal than a plain date. Copy the lines with the button under the result and paste them into a message to your partner. The tools page holds more couple tools when you want a second idea.

Set a reminder for the next anniversary the moment you see the countdown. A phone reminder a week ahead gives you time to book a table or order a gift, while a same day reminder often comes too late. The wedding hashtag generator can help you tag the photos when the day arrives.

Take a screenshot of the result and save it with your photos from that year. A saved count becomes a small time capsule, and looking back at it next year shows how far you've come. The number grows more meaningful each time you revisit it.

Match the gift to the year when the milestone is a round one. The first year is paper, the fifth is wood, the tenth is tin, the twenty fifth is silver, and the fiftieth is gold, so the year itself can pick the gift. A paper gift for year one can be a letter or a photo book, which often means more than anything bought in a rush.

Mistakes to avoid

Don't mix up your dating anniversary and your wedding anniversary when you share the number. Each date marks a different start, and using the wrong one can confuse family or spark a needless debate. Pick the date you both celebrate and stick with it every year.

Check the count a month early instead of waiting until the day itself. An anniversary calculator only helps when you look ahead, so use the countdown to plan while you still have time. A rushed gift ordered at midnight rarely beats a planned one.

Let the tool handle the calendar math instead of counting by hand. Leap years and month lengths trip up hand counts, which is why a hand count often lands a day or two off.
