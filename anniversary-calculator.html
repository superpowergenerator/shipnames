Anniversary Calculator: Years, Months, and Days Together

Enter your date and see your time together in years, months, and days.

Some dates stay with you forever, and this anniversary calculator turns yours into a number you can celebrate. Enter the day you got together or married, and see how long you have shared.

It works out the years, months, and days to today, and it also finds your next anniversary and counts the days left to it. That way you always know when the next celebration is coming.

Pick a date from the past, press the button, and your result appears right away.

How it works

This anniversary calculator takes one date and compares it with today. It counts full years first, then the months and days left over, so the total reads like a real age for your bond.

Your next anniversary is the same month and day in the year ahead, unless it falls today or has already passed this year. The countdown shows how many days remain until that date.

If you pick a date in the future, you will see a kind note asking for a past date instead, since an anniversary marks a day that already happened.

Frequently Asked Questions

What date should I enter?

+
Enter the day you got together or the day you married. Either date works, and the result shows how long you have shared since that day.

How is the time together counted?

+
It counts full years first, then whole months, then the days left over. That gives you a clear total like 3 years, 2 months, and 5 days.

What if my anniversary is today?

+
Then your next anniversary is today, and the countdown shows 0 days. That calls for a celebration right now.

Is this calculator free?

+
Yes, it is free, and your date stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere or saved.
