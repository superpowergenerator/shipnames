Rock Paper Scissors for Couples

Rock, paper, scissors against the computer. Win the best of 5 and you pick the next date.

Rock paper scissors settles small debates fast, and it gets sweeter with a prize. In rock paper scissors for couples, you play against the computer in a best of 5 match.

Win the match and the game will show you a random date idea from a list of twelve. The overall winner picks the next date, so a quick game can plan a lovely night.

You 0 : 0 Computer (first to 3 wins)

Tap Rock, Paper, or Scissors to play the first round.

How to play

Tap Rock, Paper, or Scissors in the rock paper scissors for couples game below. The computer picks at random, and the game shows both picks and who won the round.

First to three round wins takes the match, and the winner sees a date idea. Tap Play again to start a fresh match with scores back at zero.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you win rock paper scissors for couples?

+
Win three rounds first to take the best of 5 match. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock.

What happens when I win the match?

+
The game shows a random date idea from a list of 12, and the overall winner picks the next date idea.

What happens on a tie round?

+
A tie round gives no point to either side. Play another round until someone reaches three wins.

Can I play more than one match?

+
Yes. Tap Play again after a match ends, and the scores reset for a fresh best of 5.
