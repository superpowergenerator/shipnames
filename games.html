Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download anything to play?

+
No. Every game on this page runs in your browser on your phone or computer. Just open a game and play.

Are these games good for a date night?

+
Yes. They are made for two people, and most take five to fifteen minutes. Truth or dare and would you rather are good picks for a first game night.

Can I play these games with friends too?

+
Yes, most of them work with a small group as well. Never have I ever and this or that are fun with four people, and the dice game works with any number of players.
