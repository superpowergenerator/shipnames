How to read your signs

Each sign covers a set span of dates in the year, and the tool reads your birthday against those spans. Aries runs March 21 to April 19, Taurus runs April 20 to May 20, and the rest follow in order through Pisces, which runs February 19 to March 20. If you were born on March 21, try Aries first, since that's the first day of its span. Wikipedia's list of astrological signs shows every date span in one place, so check yours there if you're unsure.

Fire covers Aries, Leo, and Sagittarius; earth covers Taurus, Virgo, and Capricorn; air covers Gemini, Libra, and Aquarius; and water covers Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces. The star sign compatibility calculator scores the same element at 90, fire with air or earth with water at 82, and the tenser mixes lower. Elements drive the score, so two signs that share one tend to top the list. The zodiac groups those signs by element, so the element behind your sign drives the score.

If you were born on a cusp day, try both signs in the star sign compatibility calculator next to each other and see which reading feels closer. Cusp dates can vary by year, so the sign on the edge may flip, and the tool lets you pick signs straight from the list when a birthday lands on the line. A quick check of both options beats guessing.

Mistakes to avoid

Don't treat the score as proof about your bond. A star sign compatibility calculator is fun, and there's no proof that birth dates decide how two people get along. Trust your real talks and shared life instead, and let the stars stay in the background.

Keep a low mix out of disagreements, since a playful contrast isn't a flaw in either person. A fire and water score of 55 describes a lively contrast, and it isn't a flaw in either person, and pulling it into a real argument only adds noise. If a talk gets tense, put the tool away and listen first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my star sign from my birthday?

+
Match your birth month and day to the sign date spans on this page. If you were born on the first or last day of a span, check both signs, since cusp dates can vary by year.

What are the four star sign elements?

+
Fire covers Aries, Leo, and Sagittarius. Earth covers Taurus, Virgo, and Capricorn. Air covers Gemini, Libra, and Aquarius. Water covers Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces.

Do the same elements always match best?

+
In this tool, yes. The same element scores 90, and fire with air or earth with water scores 82. Other mixes score lower, but a lower score is still just a playful result.

Is star sign compatibility real?

+
There is no proof that birth dates decide how two people get along. Treat this tool as fun, and trust your real talks and shared life instead.
