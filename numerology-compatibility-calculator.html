How it works

Each letter has a set value: A is 1, B is 2, C is 3, D is 4, E is 5, F is 6, G is 7, H is 8, I is 9, and the pattern repeats from J onward, so J is 1, K is 2, and so on through Z as 8. Add up the letters in a name to get its total.

Then add the digits of that total again and again until you reach a single digit, with one key exception: master numbers 11 and 22 stay as they are and don't get reduced further. That final number is the life path number for that name.

The match score comes from the pair of numbers. The same number scores 92, numbers one apart score 78, classic pairings like 1 with 8, 2 with 7, and 3 with 6 score 70, and other pairs score 60. Try the numerology compatibility calculator with full names and nicknames to see how the numbers shift.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a life path number?

+
It is a single digit, or a master number 11 or 22, found by adding the letter values in a name and reducing the total by adding its digits. Fans of numerology link each number to a set of traits.

Why do 11 and 22 stay unreduced?

+
In numerology, 11 and 22 are master numbers with their own meanings, so they are not reduced to 2 and 4. The tool keeps them as they are.

Does the match percentage predict my future?

+
No. It is a fun score based on the two numbers and a simple lookup table. It does not predict real life, and it should not guide big choices.

Should I use my full name or a nickname?

+
Try both. A full name and a nickname often give different numbers, and the one you use every day may feel closer to how you see yourself.
