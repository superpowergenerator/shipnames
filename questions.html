Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use these question lists?

Pick a list that fits the mood, then take turns asking one question at a time. There is no rush, and you can skip any question that does not feel right.

Are these questions good for new couples?

Yes. The funny list is the best place to start when you are still getting to know each other. The deep and romantic lists fit better once you are comfortable talking openly.

Can I use these questions on a first date?

The funny questions work well on a first date. Save the deep and romantic ones for later, when you both know you want to keep talking.
