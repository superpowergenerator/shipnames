Newlywed Game Questions: 40 Fun Questions for Couples

Draw one random question at a time, or browse all 40 below.

Game night, a long drive, or a quiet evening at home, this set of newlywed game questions fits them all. Press the button for one fresh question at a time, or browse the full list below.

The questions cover daily life, dreams, and plenty of silly moments. They work best when you both answer honestly and laugh at the misses as much as the hits.

Play it as a couple, or bring it to a party and let each pair guess what their partner would say.

Press the button for your first question.

About Each Other

  1. What is my favorite meal of all time?
  2. What is the first thing I do when I wake up?
  3. What is my biggest pet peeve?
  4. What song always makes me want to dance?
  5. What is my dream job, if money did not matter?
  6. What is my favorite way to spend a rainy day?
  7. What food could I never give up?
  8. What is my most-used phrase?
  9. What is my favorite season, and why?
  10. What is one thing I am secretly good at?

Daily Life Together

  1. Who is more likely to burn dinner?
  2. Who takes longer to get ready in the morning?
  3. Who is the messier one at home?
  4. Who falls asleep first on the sofa?
  5. Who is better at fixing things around the house?
  6. Who picks the film on movie night?
  7. Who is more likely to lose the car keys?
  8. Who does the best impression of the other?
  9. Who snores louder?
  10. Who is the early bird, and who loves a lie-in?

Dreams and Plans

  1. Where would we live if we could live anywhere?
  2. What is one place we must visit together one day?
  3. What is a goal we should chase together this year?
  4. What would our dream home look like?
  5. What is one skill we should learn together?
  6. What would we do with a free weekend and no chores?
  7. What is our dream holiday, in three words?
  8. What is one thing we should do more often as a couple?
  9. Where do you see us in five years?
  10. What is a small dream we could make real this month?

Fun and Silly

  1. If I were a cartoon character, who would I be?
  2. What is the silliest thing I have ever done?
  3. If we were in a talent show, what would our act be?
  4. What is my worst habit, said with love?
  5. If I could talk to animals, which one would I chat with first?
  6. What is the funniest thing that ever happened to us?
  7. If we swapped lives for a day, what would go wrong first?
  8. What is my secret superpower?
  9. What would our couple theme song be?
  10. If I were a flavor of ice cream, which one would I be?

How it works

These newlywed game questions come in four groups: about each other, daily life together, dreams and plans, and fun and silly. Each group has 10 questions, for 40 in all.

Press Next question to draw one at random. You will not see the same question twice until you have seen all 40, and then the list shuffles and starts again.

The full list sits below the button, so you can pick a favorite group or jump to any question you like.

After the game, make a keepsake with the ship name generator and blend your names into one.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play the newlywed game?

+
Each partner answers a question about the other, or guesses what their partner would say. Match the answers for a point, and laugh together at the misses.

How many questions are there?

+
There are 40 questions in four groups of 10: about each other, daily life together, dreams and plans, and fun and silly.

Can we play this at a party?

+
Yes, it works well with a group. Let each couple take turns, and let the room guess along before the answers are shared.

Do the questions repeat?

+
The Next question button shows each question once before it reshuffles the list, so you will not see a repeat until you have seen all 40.
