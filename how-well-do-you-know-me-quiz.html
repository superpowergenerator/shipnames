How Well Do You Know Me Quiz for Couples

Ten playful questions about your partner, from favorites to dreams.

Think you know your partner inside out? This how well do you know me quiz puts that to a playful test, with questions about favorites, habits, and dreams.

The best answers here are the most thoughtful ones, the choices that show you have been paying attention. A high score means you notice the small things, and a low score just means you have fun questions still to ask.

Take it on your own first, then swap roles and let your partner take it too. Comparing notes is half the fun.

Question 1 of 10

How it works

This how well do you know me quiz asks 10 questions, each with four options and one best answer. You will see one question at a time, and your score out of 10 appears at the end.

Scores fall into three bands: 8 to 10 means you know them well, 5 to 7 means you know the big things, and 0 to 4 means it is time for more questions together.

Use any miss as a prompt for a real talk. Ask your partner about that topic and listen well, so next time you will know the answer for sure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this quiz work?

+
You answer 10 questions about your partner, one at a time. Each question has one best answer, and your score out of 10 appears at the end.

Can my partner take it too?

+
Yes, that is the best way to play it. Swap roles and compare scores, then talk about any answers that surprised you.

What is a good score?

+
A score of 8 or more means you know them well. Any score is fine, though, because the quiz is a fun prompt for learning more about each other.

Is this quiz only for couples?

+
It works best for couples, but close friends can play it too. Just answer about the person you know best.
