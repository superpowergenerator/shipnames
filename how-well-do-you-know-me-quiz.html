How Well Do You Know Me Quiz for Couples

Ten playful questions about your partner, from favorites to dreams.

You can finish each other's sentences at dinner, yet a small question about their favorite comfort food can still catch you out. This how well do you know me quiz puts what you know to a playful test, with questions about favorites, habits, and dreams. Take it on your own first, then swap roles and let your partner take it too.

What your score means

Your score in this how well do you know me quiz runs out of 10, and each band tells a simple story about how closely you've been paying attention. A score of 8 to 10 means you know them well, a score of 5 to 7 means you know the big things, and a score of 0 to 4 means you've got fun questions still to ask. No band is a verdict on your bond, since knowing someone is a skill you build one talk at a time.

High scorers tend to notice small things, like the drink they reach for first in the morning or the joke that always makes them smile. They remember the dream their partner mentions when they talk about the future, and they know which surprises feel thoughtful and which feel too much. If you scored high, you've likely spent time listening when it would have been easier to look at your phone.

A lower score often points to a busy season, not a lack of care, since work, kids, and long days can crowd out the small talks that keep you close. You may know their big dreams yet miss their new favorite food or the small habit that cheers them after a hard day. That gap is easy to close, and the next section shows you just how to do it. Start with the topics you missed, and you'll close the gap fast.

What to do with your score

Use your result from this how well do you know me quiz as a list of talks to have, not as a grade to argue about. Go through each question you missed and ask your partner about that topic, then listen well and remember what they share. A miss about their ideal weekend can turn into a plan for Saturday, and a miss about their proudest moment can turn into a story you'll both keep.

Swap roles and let your partner take it too, then compare scores and talk about any answers that surprised you. You may find you know their tastes well but miss their worries, or they know your habits yet miss your dreams. Comparing notes is half the fun, and it shows you both where to pay closer attention next week.

Ask one new question each week, and pick questions that go past favorites into feelings and hopes. You could ask what made their week hard, what they're most proud of right now, or what they'd pick for a perfect evening out. If you want more prompts, try the love language quiz to learn how they feel most loved, or the is he the one quiz to weigh trust and future plans. Keep a note of what you learn, so next time you'll know the answer for sure.

Mistakes to avoid

Don't treat a low score in a how well do you know me quiz as proof that you don't care, or a high score as proof that you know it all. People keep growing and changing, so a favorite from last year may have changed, and a dream from last month may have grown. A quiz can only test what you knew when you took it, and it can't test what your partner hasn't told you yet.

Keep the quiz light, not like a test at school, with right answers to pass and wrong ones to fail. The point is to learn about each other, not to win, so keep the tone light and let them laugh at a miss. If an answer stings, pause and ask why it matters to them, instead of defending your guess.

Watch out for guessing your partner's answers from what you'd pick for yourself, since that trap catches even close couples. Their ideal weekend may look nothing like yours, and their comfort food may be a dish you'd never order. Frequently Asked Questions

How does this quiz work?

+
You answer 10 questions about your partner, one at a time. Each question has one best answer, and your score out of 10 appears at the end.

Can my partner take it too?

+
Yes, that is the best way to play it. Swap roles and compare scores, then talk about any answers that surprised you.

What is a good score?

+
A score of 8 or more means you know them well. Any score is fine, though, because the quiz is a fun prompt for learning more about each other.

Is this quiz only for couples?

+
It works best for couples, but close friends can play it too. Just answer about the person you know best.
